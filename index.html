The amazing joy of reading Books!

An insider's understanding of this beautiful habit

I have absolutely loved reading books since time immemorial. As a child growing up in a nuclear family, as an adolescent charting a path through the unknown and through disparities, and finally as an adult understanding the meaning of life, books have been nothing less than amazing friends.

And these are my reasons for thinking so:

  • They are available to you all the times you are available to them.
  • The battery on them never run low on them.
  • They never hide anything from you (unless of course when it is a suspense thriller!)
  • They are genuinely okay if you do not reach out to them for a day, or a week, or even twenty years. You will find them waiting for you just the way they were when you closed the book.

What are your reasons for wanting to read a book???
